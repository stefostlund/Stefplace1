Step 1. Conconi test. Warm up 20 min. Run several steps at 4min and rest 3min. Start with slow speed, like jogg and run until HR bpm has flattened out at this speed. Increase speed by 1 km/h and repeat. Enter speed and HR bpm. Note that my personal data has to deleted one by one. My data is there for you to see how it works. This line will be linear up to the LTHR where you start to produce as much Lactate that it will impare your performance and HR are not increasing as fast anymore. If not detected the app will use your highest HR in the test. The relation between and HR will also be used as starting values for step 3 race data so this test should be done close to your race and in conditions as close as possible to the race day.
Step 2. Plot the relation between your race times and average heart rate HR in bpm ( beats per min). In best case are they close to linear and good enough up to a marathon but in my case is the long races underestimated and the non-linear second equation will fit better. LTHR for different race times are calculated. The Conconi LTHR from step 1 should be around the LTHR30min. If you have races by your own please delete my races and put in your own. If you don't you can click "adjust to Conconi" and your Conconi LTHR will move my plotted curve to your level based on the 30min test and is assuming that you and me will have the same slope, just different LTHR levels which never been tested.
Put in the distance you will race and step 1 and 2 will provide a starting HR, starting speed and calculated finish time by iterating the HR and time along the step 2 plotted line including the relation between speed and HR. E.g assuming 4h finish time give a bpm of 159 bpm on the step 2 line. 4h requires a speed of 10,55 km/h. Step 1 says however that 159 bpm just give 9.5km/h and a longer finish time. The new finish time is 4,44h 4h26min. 4 44h gives a new lower bpm and an even longer time. The iteration finds zoom the start values of speed and bpm.
Race day. Check what actual speed you get when running at start bpm. If lower than start value decrease bpm to a new longer time or prepare your self for hard ending of the race.
